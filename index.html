Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our mountain tree deliveries.

What does "Hands-Free / Effort-Free" setup include?

We bring the tree inside your home or office, place it securely into your tree stand (or one supplied by us), adjust and level it, unwrap the netting, and sweep up any loose needles before leaving.

How does delivery pricing work?

Delivery is 100% FREE for all trees 9ft and over! For trees 8ft and under, a flat $25 Peninsula/South Bay distance surcharge applies.

What tree species do you offer?

We harvest premium Noble Firs (strong branches, classic scent), Grand Firs (lush green, sweet citrus aroma), and Douglas Firs directly from local Santa Cruz Mountain farms.

How does post-holiday haul-away work?

If you select the $100 January haul-away add-on, we will schedule a pickup date in early January to dismantle the tree indoors, bag it, carry it out cleanly, and haul it to a green recycling facility.
